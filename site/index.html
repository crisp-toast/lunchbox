Questions, answered.

What does Tessel need permission for?

Accessibility, so it can move and resize other apps' windows and listen for its shortcuts. Screen Recording is optional: allow it and the Shelf in Focus shows live previews of your windows instead of app icons.

Will it take over my keyboard?

Only a handful of combinations, and only with the left ⌘ key. Right ⌘ keeps doing what it always did, so ⌘← still jumps to the start of a line.

Can I change the shortcuts?

Yes, every one. Open Keyboard Shortcuts from the menu bar, click a command and press the keys you want. Tessel turns down combinations that would get in the way of typing or that another command already uses, and Restore Defaults puts everything back.

How do I set it up?

Tessel walks you through it the first time it opens: allow Accessibility, tap the three keys you want for your Workspaces, and try every shortcut on a practice screen that can't touch your real windows. It takes about a minute, and you can run it again from the menu bar.

How do the three Workspaces work?

During setup you tap three keys you never use, ideally out of the way on the right, like Page Up, Page Down and End. ⌘ plus one of them switches Workspaces instantly; add ⇧ to take the focused window with you. Stash (⇧⌘H) sends a window to Workspace 3 without leaving the one you're in.

Does it work with more than one display?

Yes. Each display has its own layout and focus crosses between them at the edges. If a second display is a dummy plug or you only want your main screen tiled, turn off Use All Displays in Settings.
