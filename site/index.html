Questions

What does Tessel need permission for?

Accessibility, so it can move and resize other apps' windows and listen for its shortcuts. Screen Recording is optional: allow it and the Shelf in Focus shows live previews of your windows instead of app icons.

Will it take over my keyboard?

Only the left ⌘ key's arrow shortcuts, plus the few combinations listed in the help. Right ⌘ keeps doing what it always did, so ⌘← still jumps to the start of a line. Esc only goes to Tessel while Focus is on.

How do I set it up?

Tessel walks you through it the first time it opens: allow Accessibility, tap the three keys you want for your Spaces, and try the essentials once. It takes about a minute, and you can run it again from the menu bar.

How do the three Spaces work?

During setup you tap three keys you never use, ideally out of the way on the right, like Page Up, Page Down and End. ⌘ plus one of them switches Spaces instantly; add ⇧ to take the focused window with you. Stash (⌘⇧H) sends a window to Space 3 without leaving.

Does it work with more than one display?

Yes. Each display has its own layout and focus crosses between them at the edges. If a second display is a dummy plug or you only want your main screen tiled, turn off Use All Displays in Settings.
